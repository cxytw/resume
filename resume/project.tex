  \cventry
    {Master's Thesis Project, NTUST  |  IASDR 2025 Best Paper Award  |  Advisor: Prof. Hsien-Hui Tang} % Position
    {Temple Service Digitalization --- Independent UX Research \& Service Design Project} % Title
    {Yilan, Taiwan} % Location
    {Feb 2024 -- Jun 2026} % Date(s)
    {
      \vspace{-4.0mm}
      \begin{cvitems}
        \item {Led a 0-to-1 digital transformation project, replacing paper-based, experience-dependent workflows with a trackable, maintainable digital service for a Taoist temple.}
        \item {Achieved a 52.2\% digital migration rate across 9,200+ users(as of July 2026) and 6,100+ service records, accelerating service payment collection to over NT\$10M during a single event; improved user satisfaction from 4.0/5 to 4.4/5.}
        \item {Diagnosed systemic service breakdowns through 17 stakeholder interviews and service journey mapping, uncovering 11 pain points and 4 structural gaps, and architected an end-to-end platform spanning back-office management and public-facing registration, consolidating 18 manual process nodes into 6.}
        \item {The new system cut single-record processing time from 1 week to under 5 minutes, turning a fully manual, staff-dependent process into a trackable digital system.}
      \end{cvitems}
