    {Master's Thesis Project, NTUST  |  IASDR 2025 Best Paper Award  |  Advisor: Prof. Hsien-Hui Tang} % Position
    {Temple Service Digitalization --- Independent UX Research \& Service Design Project} % Title
    {} % Location
    {Feb 2024 -- Jun 2026} % Date(s)
    {
      \vspace{-2mm}
      \begin{cvitems}
        \item {Independently led research, design, and implementation of a 0-to-1 digital service system for a Taoist temple, replacing paper-based, experience-dependent workflows with a trackable, maintainable digital service.}
        \item {Conducted stakeholder interviews, service journey mapping, workflow analysis, and pain-point synthesis; identified 11 service pain points and 4 structural gaps, translating findings into product requirements and interface design.}
        \item {Designed an integrated service system spanning back-office management, online registration, and LINE-based notifications, defining role permissions and an online-offline integration strategy.}
        \item {Achieved a 52.2\% digital migration rate across 9,000+ users and 6,100+ service records; cut back-office processing from days to 2--3 hours and user submission time to 1--2 minutes.}
        \item {Improved user satisfaction from 4.0/5 to 4.4/5; findings presented at IASDR 2025 and awarded Best Paper.}
      \end{cvitems}
