    {Product Designer / Service Designer / UX Researcher} % Role
    {Temple Service Digitalization Project} % Title
    {Yilan, Taiwan} % Location
    {Apr 2024 -- Feb 2026} % Date(s)
    {
      \begin{cvitems} % Description(s) of tasks/responsibilities
        \item {Led the design and implementation of a 0-to-1 digital service system, transforming paper-based, manual, and experience-dependent workflows into a trackable and maintainable digital service.}
        \item {Conducted stakeholder interviews, service journey analysis, pain point synthesis, and requirement definition to support product planning and system implementation.}
        \item {Designed an integrated service workflow across a back-office management system, online registration flow, and LINE official account notification channel.}
        \item {Identified 11 service pain points and 4 structural gaps, informing design strategies for role permissions, information feedback, online-offline integration, and real-time data synchronization.}
        \item {Achieved a 52.2\% digital service migration rate, supported 9,000+ LINE official account users, and enabled 6,100+ completed orders after implementation.}
        \item {Reduced back-office data processing from several days to 2--3 hours, while shortening user registration and data submission from cross-day waiting to 1--2 minutes.}
        \item {Improved system user satisfaction from 4.0/5 to 4.5/5, an increase of approximately 12.5\%.}
        \item {Research outcome received the IASDR 2025 Best Paper Award and was presented at the International Association of Societies of Design Research Conference.}
      \end{cvitems}
    }
